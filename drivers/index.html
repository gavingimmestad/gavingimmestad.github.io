Drivers of climate change

There are numerous reasons for climate change, amongst the highest are greenhouse gas emissions and pollution. Alot if not most, of the responsibility for the negative climate change we see today lies in the hands of private institutions. The government has a lot of effect on the decisions of institutions, making decisions that affect the climate, for example, The Paris agreement limits global average temperatures to 1.5 °C annually. The developed nations' commitment pledges to mobilize $100 billion per year in climate finance, and many countries have committed to zero net emissions by the second half of the 21st century.
