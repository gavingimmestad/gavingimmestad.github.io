Impacts of climate change

Everyone is affected by climate change, even if you can't notice it. The Earth is getting hotter, the sea is getting warmer, and storms are becoming more frequent and stronger. Climate change affects animals too; in 2010-2019 alone, more than 150 species went extinct, 2024 has been the hottest year ever recorded, and our ocean is on track to become a carbon sink, and floods and hurricanes have increased greatly in frequency since 1980.
