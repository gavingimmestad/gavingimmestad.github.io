My View of the future of climate change.

Throughout my time here at App State, I have found that we have reached the point of no return for many issues surrounding climate change. We will never be able to bring the sea levels back down, and we will never be able to recover from the damage we have done to our coral reefs. Does this mean that we should give up and exploit the other aspects of our environment? Absolutely not, we need to take as many measures as possible to reverse the damage being done currently. Some things we can do to slow or prevent further damage to our climate include promoting more environmentally wise thinking in institutional decision making and pushing for more eco-friendly energy policies.
